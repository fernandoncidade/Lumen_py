RELEASE NOTES — Lúmen
Version: 2026.2.1.0
Date: February 1, 2026
Author: Fernando Nillsson Cidade

Overview
- Update aimed at greater consistency of texts, better adaptation between light/dark themes, increased robustness of the Accessible Reader and Feynman Method interfaces, and centralization of menu translations. Defensive measures were added to avoid failures at critical points and logs were improved for diagnostics.

Main highlights
- Languages and texts: interface texts were centralized and now update at runtime while preserving user selections and states.
- Theme and colors: editing elements and lists automatically adjust their colors to maintain adequate contrast when switching themes.
- Visual fallbacks: when custom controls are not available, the app uses standard controls to keep functionality and a coherent appearance.
- Font management: font/size selection more resilient to invalid configurations; more stable reading of user preferences.
- Accessible Reader: refined toolbar with load, playback (play/pause/stop) controls, speed and volume adjustment with visual feedback, integrated search, and more reliable PDF selection copy behavior.
- Feynman Method: interface reorganized into tabs that guide the flow (Concept → Explain → Gaps → Review), more consistent buttons (New/Save/Delete/Clear) and better theme adaptation.
- Main menu: texts and shortcuts standardized across the menu, with common key combinations for frequent tasks.
- Logs and stability: standardized error and debug messages to facilitate problem investigation.

Bug fixes
- Updating tab texts is now safe, avoiding crashes when changing language.
- Contrast and element clipping issues across different themes were fixed.
- Buttons and selectors work correctly even without optional modules installed.
- Shortcuts and key contexts were adjusted to avoid inconsistent behavior on different platforms.
- Copying selection in PDFs received protections to avoid silent failures.

What changes in daily use
- Changing the language updates texts immediately without losing your selections.
- Switching the theme (light/dark) preserves readability of editing areas and lists.
- The Reader shows clearer controls for playback, search and copying PDF excerpts.
- The Feynman Method is easier to use with guided tabs and a Clear button always available.
- Fewer unexpected errors in environments missing optional components.

Quick recommendations
- Try switching language and theme to check immediate updates of texts and colors.
- In the Reader, test speed/volume and search to validate the new experience.
- In the Feynman Method, create/edit a concept and use the Clear button to check the flow.
- If you encounter odd behavior, consult the logs for clues before reporting.

Overview
- Improvements to the Mind Maps feature: faster and more stable import, automatic node organization, layout preservation and more reliable interactions. Text normalization fixes were also applied.

- Ability to save a layout "snapshot" and restore the original view whenever desired.
- Auxiliary nodes to highlight main ideas, with visual links and importance indicators.
- More stable interactions: dragging, clicking and connecting nodes work better and preserve positions when moved.
- Texts detected in documents undergo normalization and automatic correction of common PT/EN terms.
- Addendum: Feynman Method improvements — dynamic translations, preservation of the domain combo selection, robust tooltip generation and consistent use of the button manager with fallbacks.

Daily usage improvements
- Reorganizing maps with AI is more predictable; you can revert to the previous state easily (snapshot/restore).
- Large imports are less likely to hang and show progress; cancellation properly cleans up threads.
- Map visualization is cleaner and more readable, even with many nodes; radial placement with overlap detection reduces collisions.
- Less loss of position data when rearranging items; click and drag behavior is more consistent.
- Addendum: texts and labels adapt to the runtime language without losing state; more detailed logs ease diagnostics.

Quick recommendations
- When using AI import, monitor progress and cancel if necessary.
- Test theme/language reactions to confirm texts and placeholders.
- If you encounter unexpected behavior, ensure you're on the latest version.
- Addendum: validate worker cancellation at different stages and check logs in source/utils/LogManager for useful evidence when reporting bugs.

RELEASE NOTES — Lúmen
Version: 2025.12.23.0
Date: December 23, 2025
Author: Fernando Nillsson Cidade

Overview
Update focused on making the Eisenhower Matrix calendar and panel more informative, visual and resilient, and on strengthening the application's sound management (selection, preview and alarm repetition). Appearance adjustments were made to avoid artifacts when switching themes and robustness improvements reduce failures in adverse scenarios.

Key changes
- More useful calendar
  - Days with tasks show visual counters (badges) per day/week/month.
  - Dates with tasks are visually highlighted; filters allow viewing items by Day, Week or Month.
  - The calendar task list is filtered and ordered according to the selected period; shows a placeholder when empty.
  - Navigation and panel resizing tools were improved for more predictable behavior.

- Appearance synchronization
  - Internal components (lists, fields and viewports) adapt better to the app palette, reducing visual artifacts when switching light/dark themes.
  - Text and date/time formats are reapplied when changing language without requiring restart.

- Controls and visual details
  - The side toggle (rotated tab) and field outlines were adjusted for more stable painting and sizing, preventing clipping and artifacts.
  - Small style improvements ensure a consistent look across modules.

- Enhanced sound management
  - A list of available sounds can be chosen with quick preview.
  - Sound preference and repeat interval are saved between sessions.
  - Repetitive alarms behave predictably: audio plays, waits (sound duration + configured interval) and repeats until stopped.
  - The stop action interrupts playback and repetition immediately; previewing does not change the persisted selection.
  - Fallbacks and invalid-file handling ensure safe behavior even when a sound is missing or corrupted.

Robustness and logs
- Multiple protections were added to prevent painting, locale, palette or audio errors from freezing the UI.
- Logs were expanded to ease diagnosis of issues without impacting the user experience.

Quick checks
1. Open the Eisenhower Matrix and create tasks with dates/times.
2. Open the calendar panel/dialog:
   - Verify badges on dates and highlights for dates with tasks.
   - Switch between Day/Week/Month and confirm the list is filtered/ordered.
3. Change language and confirm texts and date/time formats update.
4. Switch theme (light/dark) and check lists and dropdowns do not show artifacts.
5. Test sound:
   - Open sound settings, preview a sound, save the selection and start a repeating alarm; confirm it repeats at the expected interval and stopping halts the cycle.
   - Test behavior with a missing or invalid sound to confirm safe fallback.

What changes for you
- A more informative calendar that visually matches the app theme.
- Better experience when switching language and theme.
- More predictable sound alarms, with preview, saved preference and controlled repetition until confirmation.
